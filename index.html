ANGULAR MASKS
















Angular rings
Default rings are 10, 20, 30... degrees from center. A 60-degree cone diameter lies on the 30-degree radius ring. Spacing follows the confirmed projection; it is not equal pixel spacing.


40 degrees total means 20 degrees above and 20 below the gaze horizon, spanning the captured horizontal field. Gaze angles are relative to the source optical axis; roll sets the horizon. Guth outlines are reference envelopes, not a glare position-index map or an individual's perimetry.
